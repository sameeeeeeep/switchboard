## The Cap playbook, applied

Cap never leads with its stack. It leads with **"the open source alternative to Loom"** — a category anchor everyone already understands — then outcomes ("product demos, bug reports, onboarding"), and keeps one drumbeat under everything: **own your data** ("use Cap Cloud, connect your own S3, keep recordings local, or self-host"). The repo reads like a product homepage, not documentation.

Mapped to us:

| Cap move | Our move |
| --- | --- |
| Category anchor: "open source alternative to Loom" | Anchor each app to a job people already pay for: brandbrain = the brand studio/agency deliverable |
| Outcomes before features | Show the artifact: the brand system, the deck, the storefront — never the architecture first |
| "Own your data" drumbeat | "Runs on the AI you already pay for" drumbeat — Switchboard is the one line at the end, every time |
| Repo as product homepage | Every post ends at the app, and the app carries the Switchboard chip |

The platform is never the headline. It is the last line of every post, always the same shape: *free and open source, runs on your existing setup through Switchboard.*

**One rule carried over from the bank:** honest register. No "revolutionary", no fabricated numbers, "supported" hedges kept, provider limits acknowledged where relevant.

v4 — the OS direction

v3 is a store you shop in. v4 is a desk your work sits on. Same tokens, same five registers, same type scale — the change is what occupies the top of the page. The catalogue does not disappear; it recedes to the bottom, because in an OS you do not browse the Applications folder, you work on your stuff.

The one new component is THE QUEUE. Every wrapp stages work and nothing irreversible happens without you — that is already true, per wrapp, invisibly. The OS's job is to collect it: everything staged across every app, in one list, each tagged auto / needs you / yours. It promotes Autopilot's mode taxonomy from a wrapp detail to a system concept, and it is the demo line: "five things are staged across four apps. Not one of them happened without me."

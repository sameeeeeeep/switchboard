01 · what shows where

The surface ladder

A progressive-disclosure ladder anchored at the notch seam. Each rung shows more and costs more attention; each escalates to the next by one gesture. The must-not column is the anti-clutter rule that keeps a glance a glance.

02 · shared primitives

The atoms every wrapp inherits

A wrapp fills these; it never redefines them. That is why 65 wrapps read as one system — consistency by construction, not by review.

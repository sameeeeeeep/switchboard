# Content Strategy — The Last Prompt

*How we turn the [Direction](./DIRECTION.md) into a steady stream of content. The playbook: who
it's for, what we make, where it lives, and how often.*

---

## Objective

Make **The Last Prompt** legible and magnetic to the people who'd actually use Switchboard and
believe the promptless thesis — then convert attention into `Enter →`.

## Audience

Builders, founders, PM-minded operators, and AI-curious tinkerers. They're moved by *"wait, it does
that?"* and by honesty — not by hype or by cute.

## Content pillars (mapped to the vision ladder)

| Pillar | Ladders to | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Vision / manifesto** | Build sci-fi (4) | The Sam Kurzwala story; the promptless thesis; "what MCP did for connectors…" |
| **Routing / how it works** | Routable components (1) | Switchboard explainers: the broker, the five noes, the reverse flow, BYO model |
| **Proof (wrapps + Half-Baked)** | Substrate works | Build-in-public: audience votes a half-baked wrapp, we ship it live |
| **Founder / ADHD** | Why us | PM-who-couldn't-code, ADHD-as-operating-system — the human spine |

## Format: articles with a branded hero

- **Canonical home:** the blog at **thelastprompt.ai/journey.** The blog is the real copy; SEO and
  the archive stay on our own domain.
- **Each article** ships with: a branded hero image + the full piece on /journey.
- **Social = distribution, not the work.** LinkedIn gets a 3-line teaser linking back; X/Threads
  gets a thread summarizing it and linking back.

### House image style (so every hero is one brand)
Typographic, dark, not stock-photo. Ground `#07080A`/`#0A0A0B`; one accent — lime `#9FCB6E`; type
**IBM Plex Mono** (lab) or **Space Grotesk + Doto** (Switchboard); motifs = the menu-bar+notch, the
board, the two hands. Heroes are **designed graphics rendered HTML→PNG** (free, perfectly
consistent). Template + first hero live in `content/articles/images/`.

## The engine: "Half-Baked" (recurring)

Most of the ~94 wrapps are unfinished — that's ADHD, and we say so. The incompleteness *is* the
format. Weekly cycle:

1. **Reveal** — show 3–4 half-baked wrapps (badged *unfinished* in the store), audience votes.
2. **Build** — winner built live on our own Claude (doubles as a Switchboard/reverse-flow demo).
3. **Ship** — store badge flips to done, free to run.

Rules: never oversell an unfinished wrapp; only the winner gets finished; the finished bar is real.
Product prereq: an *unfinished* badge in the store + Backlog "Finish X" cards.

## Voice

Lowercase, self-deprecating, ADHD-as-throughline, anti "yet another account." Literary but grounded
for the manifesto; punchy and concrete for product + social. Specific beats clever.

## Cadence (example month)

Backbone = the Half-Baked weekly cycle (Reveal → Build → Ship). Around it:

- **Wk 1** — Manifesto (cornerstone) · "it's not an app, it's an app store" · the five noes
- **Wk 2** — Half-Baked launch + Reveal #1 · Flow (Wispr story) · Build/Ship #1
- **Wk 3** — Your AI, with hands (reverse flow) · BrandBrain · Reveal #2 · Build/Ship #2
- **Wk 4** — Guru / guided touch · BYO model · Reveal #3 · 30-day retro

Full day-by-day skeleton: `content/calendar-30day.md`. Article backlog: `content/articles/00-plan.md`.

## Distribution & measurement

- **Channels:** thelastprompt.ai/journey (canonical) → LinkedIn + X/Threads (teasers back).
- **Watch:** /journey reads & scroll-depth, `Enter →` clicks to Switchboard, Half-Baked vote counts
  (also = free demand validation), saves/shares on the founder/ADHD posts.
- **One rule:** every piece ends by pointing one rung further down the ladder — toward `Enter →`.

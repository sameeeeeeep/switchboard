# Switchboard — 30-day content calendar

**Backbone:** the **Half-Baked** series runs on a weekly cycle — Reveal (vote) → Build (live) →
Ship (badge flips to done). Everything else fills around that spine.

**Cadence:** 30 ideas = one per day, but you don't have to post daily. Run ~5/week, skip
weekends if you want. The Half-Baked cycle is the part you never skip — it's the engine.

**Pillars:**
- 🧠 THESIS — platform / privacy / why it exists (credibility)
- 🍞 HALF-BAKED — the series (your differentiator + demand validation)
- 👤 STORY — founder / ADHD / build-in-public (personal, high-save)
- 🔧 SPOTLIGHT — a real, built thing (proof)
- 🔥 REACH — hot take / lightweight / engagement (top-of-funnel)

**Prereqs before Day 4 launch:**
1. "Unfinished" badge live in the Store (small build).
2. First vote shortlist: 3–4 half-baked wrapps + one honest "what's missing" line each.

## Notes

- **The spine is Reveal → Build → Ship, weekly.** Four full cycles in 30 days. If a build slips
  (it will, it's ADHD — that's on-brand), the slip itself is a post: "still baking. here's where
  it's stuck." Honesty compounds; don't fake a ship.
- **Front-load the thesis (Week 1)** so newcomers land on "what is this" before the series asks
  them to vote.
- **Video days are the heavy lift.** Batch-record: one sit-down can capture Build + Ship for a
  cycle. Text/poll days are cheap — write them in a single session.
- **Reuse ruthlessly:** an X thread becomes a LinkedIn post with the breaks removed; a Build clip
  becomes the Ship post's "before." One idea → 2–3 pieces.
- **8 of these are already drafted** (Week 1 is nearly done). The ~20 ✍️ are the writing queue —
  I can knock out the text ones in batches whenever you want.

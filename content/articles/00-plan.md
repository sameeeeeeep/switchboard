# Switchboard — article series (the plan)

**Format shift:** instead of standalone posts, the month is a set of **cornerstone articles**,
each topped with a **branded hero image**. Longer, durable, linkable — and one article covers a
whole theme instead of teasing it across five posts.

**Publishing home:** the articles live on the blog at **thelastprompt.ai/journey**. LinkedIn +
X get a short teaser (3 lines / a thread) that links back to the full piece on /journey — the
blog is the canonical copy, social is distribution. This keeps SEO + the archive on your own
domain instead of renting it on LinkedIn.

**Cadence:** ~2–3 articles/week over the month. The Half-Baked "build-log" articles interleave
as wrapps actually ship.

## House image style (so every hero looks like the same brand)

Switchboard's identity is typographic and dark — NOT AI-photo. Match it:

- **Ground:** near-black `#0A0A0B`, with a soft lime radial glow in one corner.
- **Pop colour:** lime `#9FCB6E` (and deep `#5E8B23`). Used sparingly — one accent per image.
- **Type:** Space Grotesk (display) + a dot-matrix "Doto" wordmark for SWITCHBOARD.
- **Motif:** the menu-bar strip + the notch; consent cards; the kanban board. Real product
  surfaces, stylised — never generic tech clip-art.
- **Ratio:** 1200×630 (LinkedIn/OG). Big headline, lots of negative space, one accent object.

The heroes are **designed graphics** (HTML/SVG rendered to PNG), which stays on-brand, free, and
perfectly consistent — the opposite of a stock-photo look.

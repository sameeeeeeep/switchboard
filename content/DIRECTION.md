## One line

**The Last Prompt is an AI lab building science fiction.**

## The vision ladder

Read top-down it's the dream; read bottom-up it's the plan. We build from the bottom.

| # | Rung | Plain meaning |
|---|------|---------------|
| 4 | **Build science fiction** | The AI we were promised — it discovers, predicts, protects, *acts*. |
| 3 | **Self-improving, autonomous AI** | Runs on its own. Better tomorrow than today. Awake to its own goals. |
| 2 | **Promptless AI** | The first real step: an AI that no longer waits for you to type the next thing. |
| 1 | **Routable AI components** ← *shipping now* | Make every piece of AI addressable & composable, so an AI can run without a human wiring it. This is **Switchboard**. |

**The bet:** the blocker to autonomous AI isn't intelligence — the models are already capable of
far more than we let them do. The blocker is that every *piece* of AI is trapped in its own box.
Free the pieces and the higher rungs become buildable.

## The wedge: routable AI components (Path 1)

MCP made **connectors** routable — one universal way for any model to reach any tool. That was one
plug on a switchboard with a hundred sockets.

We do it for **the whole stack** — every component of intelligence, addressable / swappable /
composable:

`harnesses · interfaces · subscriptions · models · context · data · users · agents`

That is the substrate a promptless, self-improving AI stands on and *runs* — instead of a human
hand-carrying intelligence between silos, prompt by prompt.

> **The tagline for path 1:** *what MCP did for connectors, Switchboard does for everything else.*

## The name

"The last prompt," two ways:
1. **The goal** — the final thing you'll ever have to type; the prompt that makes prompting unnecessary.
2. **The work** — someone has to *write* that prompt first. That's us.

## Sam Kurzwala

The founding myth (see the manifesto). A normal person — Kurzweil's futurism × Altman's ambition ×
the Indian *"-wala"*, the one who *does* the thing — who builds a personal future-prediction AI and
discovers it can't act on the future it predicts, because it's on a leash made of prompts. Pulling
that thread reveals the whole ladder. **Sam Kurzwala is not a special person; he's what happens when
an ordinary one stops waiting for the future and starts wiring it together.** He's the reader.

## What we are / aren't

- **Are:** an AI lab with a shipping product (Switchboard) and a long thesis (promptless → autonomous → sci-fi).
- **Aren't:** another chat wrapper, another subscription, another data silo. Switchboard runs on *your*
  AI, *your* machine, no account (see Switchboard's "five noes").

## North star

Progress is measured as movement down the prompt curve: **prompts → fewer → promptless.** Every
release should remove prompts from the loop, not add them.
